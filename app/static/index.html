Your KYC applications

Indian regulations require verified business KYC before numbers can be rented in your name. Only businesses registered in India are eligible. Review is automated and usually takes about 5 minutes.

Registration documents

Download current copies from the MCA, Udyam or GST portal: they are checked against government records. The business name must be exactly the same on every document. Use a different file for each document. A business PAN alone is not accepted.

Find a number

Indian landline numbers (080, 022…) may be used for service and transactional calls only. TRAI rules prohibit promotional content on them: promotional calls need 140-series numbers. Calls also need the recipient's consent, otherwise they count as spam (UCC) and can get your KYC suspended.
